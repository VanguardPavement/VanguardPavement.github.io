About the Company

BUILT ON
QUALITY.

Vanguard Pavement Solutions is a licensed, insured commercial pavement marking company serving the greater Tampa Bay area. We started to solve a simple problem: property managers needed reliable, high-quality lot striping without the runaround.

Our Story

Why We Started

Parking lots are the first thing customers see. A faded, disorganized lot signals a property that doesn't care — and that impression sticks. We started Vanguard Pavement Solutions because we saw too many good properties let down by poor markings and worse contractors.

Our team brings hands-on experience with commercial pavement work across Tampa Bay and Central Florida. We invest in professional equipment, use quality materials, and approach every job like it matters — because to the property owner, it does.

We focus exclusively on commercial and institutional properties — parking lots, shopping centers, HOA communities, industrial facilities, and multi-family complexes. That focus means we're not spreading ourselves thin across residential driveways or general landscaping. Pavement markings are what we do, and we're good at it.

When you hire Vanguard, you get a crew that shows up on time, works clean, and delivers lines you're proud to show your clients. No excuses. No re-dos needed. Just sharp, lasting results.
