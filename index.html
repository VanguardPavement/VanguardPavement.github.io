FAQ

Common Questions

How long does parking lot striping take?
Most standard lots are completed in 2–4 hours. Larger properties or those requiring layout changes may take a full day. We'll give you a firm time estimate when we quote your job, and we always work around your schedule to minimize disruption.
How long before I can drive on fresh lines?
Traffic paint lines are typically dry enough to drive on within 30 minutes in normal Florida weather. We'll give you a clear all-clear time when the job is done. Seal coating requires a longer cure — usually 24–48 hours depending on conditions.
Do you handle ADA compliance?
Yes. We install ADA-compliant handicap spaces, van-accessible stalls, access aisles, and all required markings to current federal ADA Standards for Accessible Design. If your property is out of compliance we can assess and correct it.
How much does parking lot striping cost?
Pricing depends on the number of stalls, services required (new layout vs. re-stripe), and lot access. A typical commercial re-stripe is very affordable. We provide free, written estimates with no pressure — contact us and we'll quote your specific lot.
Do I need to close my parking lot for the job?
Not necessarily. We can often work in sections to keep part of your lot open, or schedule for early mornings, evenings, or weekends when traffic is minimal. We'll plan the approach around your business needs.
Do you stripe over existing faded lines?
Yes — re-striping over existing faded lines is one of our most common services. We use high-quality traffic paint that restores lines to full visibility with excellent durability under Florida's sun and rain.
What areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Lakeland, Sarasota, Wesley Chapel, Plant City, Riverview, New Port Richey, Bradenton, and the broader Tampa Bay and Central Florida region. Not sure if we cover your location? Just call — we travel for the right project.
How do I prepare my lot before you arrive?
Just make sure the area is clear of vehicles before we arrive. We handle all the prep, layout, and cleanup. For seal coating jobs the surface should be clean and dry — we'll advise on any specific steps when we schedule your job.
